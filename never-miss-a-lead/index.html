Questions, answered

The things owners always ask

Straight answers, no sales fog.

Does the AI sound like a robot?

No. It uses natural, conversational speech and handles interruptions, accents, and background noise. Callers regularly can't tell. You approve the exact script before we go live, and it can hand off to a human anytime.

Do I have to change my phone number?

Never. You keep your real number. We add a simple forwarding rule: if your line rings out or it's after hours, our assistant picks up instead of the voicemail. Nothing changes for callers except that someone actually answers.

How fast can this be live?

Under 7 days from your go-ahead, usually 3. Setup is done by us: number routing, scripts, calendar connection, and testing. Your team doesn't install anything.

What about patient privacy?

We treat it seriously. The assistant only collects what's needed to book: name, number, reason, preferred time. Recordings and transcripts stay in your account, and we'll sign the paperwork your compliance requires.
