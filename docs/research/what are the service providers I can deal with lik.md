# what are the service providers I can deal with like tattoo, med spa and

Think of “service providers” here as **any business that does a procedure where the body has to heal and the client gets instructions to follow at home.**

For your aftercare platform, the most relevant groups are:

### Core segments (start here)

1. **Tattoo \& Piercing Studios**
    - Services: tattoos, permanent makeup / microblading, body piercing, tattoo removal.
    - Why good: high aftercare risk, lots of anxiety, many still use paper or Instagram DMs.
2. **Med Spas / Aesthetic Clinics**
    - Botox / neurotoxins
    - Dermal fillers
    - Microneedling / RF microneedling
    - Laser hair removal
    - Skin resurfacing (CO2, IPL, fractional lasers)
    - Chemical peels
    - Body contouring (CoolSculpting, etc.)
3. **Plastic Surgery \& Cosmetic Surgery Practices**
    - Surgical procedures (lipo, BBL, breast aug, face/neck lifts).
    - Very intense aftercare, but more regulated and closer to Easy Aftercare’s core.

### Adjacent segments (later expansion)

4. **Dermatology clinics**
    - Biopsies, excisions, cryotherapy, light therapies, acne procedures.
5. **Dental \& Oral Surgery**
    - Extractions, implants, grafts, ortho adjustments.
6. **Chiropractic / Physio / Sports Medicine**
    - Rehab exercises, home device use, posture reminders.
7. **IV clinics / Wellness centers**
    - IV therapy, hormone therapy, weight‑loss injections (GLP‑1), etc.
8. **Post‑op recovery / concierge nursing**
    - Recovery houses and private nurses for plastic surgery patients.

***

If you’re validating with limited time, I’d focus your interviews on just **2 primary provider types**:

- Tattoo / PMU studios
- Small med spas / injectors

Those give you very different but complementary perspectives, and both are clearly in your strike zone.
