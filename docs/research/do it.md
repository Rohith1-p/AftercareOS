# Aftercare Automation Landscape – Positioning Notes

## Quick Summary

We are *not* the first to see this opportunity.  
Easy Aftercare and PostCare.net both validate the problem: **paper aftercare fails, clinics lose time and reviews**.

Our job is to own a **specific, underserved corner** of this space:

> **“SMS‑first, small‑practice‑friendly aftercare automation for med spas, injectors, and tattoo / PMU studios.”**

---

## Competitor Snapshot

| Company         | Channel / UX                                   | Who They Target                               | Key Strengths                                                                 | Key Weaknesses (Our Opportunities)                                                                 |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Easy Aftercare** | SMS + email + web portal                        | Broad medical + aesthetics (medspa, dental, ortho, surgery, etc.) | Deep feature set, strong compliance, lots of specialties, Zenoti integration | Expensive ($149–399/mo + $299 setup, 12‑mo contract), heavier onboarding, generic medical branding |
| **PostCare.net**  | **WhatsApp** journeys + AI assistant + photo triage | Aesthetics, medspa, dental, tattoo/body art, cosmetic surgery     | Modern UX, day‑based journeys, 70+ protocols, AI replies, concern detection  | WhatsApp‑centric (not ideal for US SMS), manual patient enrollment, more complex, likely higher priced |
| **Postcare.link** | Web aftercare pages via link (no app)           | Clinics wanting digital sheets instead of paper                    | Super light, fast to create, no login                                       | No automated, timed SMS; static info only; limited escalation / review flows                       |

---

## Our Planned Position

**Working name:** (TBD)

**One‑sentence positioning:**

> **Automated SMS aftercare for med spas, injectors, and tattoo studios – cheaper and simpler than enterprise tools, and easier than repairing a 1‑star review.**

### Target Customer

- Solo and small‑team **injectors / med spas** (1–5 providers)
- **Tattoo and PMU studios** that care about healing and Google reviews
- Primarily **US & Canada**, where SMS is default and WhatsApp penetration is uneven

### Core Differentiators

1. **SMS‑First in the US**
   - No WhatsApp requirement.
   - Works with the same phone number every client already uses.
   - Feels native to how US med‑spa and tattoo clients expect to be contacted.

2. **Square/Vagaro‑Triggered Journeys**
   - Aftercare starts automatically when an appointment is marked complete.
   - No manual patient enrollment screens after every procedure.
   - Tight focus on **Square first**, then Vagaro/Fresha.

3. **Ultra‑Simple AI Protocol Upload**
   - Upload PDF/Word/text → AI proposes a full Day 0 / Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 7 SMS sequence.
   - Preset tone options: “clinical”, “friendly med‑spa”, “tattoo‑shop voice”.
   - Built for busy small shops that will never sit through 3 hours of template setup.

4. **“Something’s Wrong” + Review Protection**
   - Every sequence includes an escalation link:
     - Collects text + photo evidence.
     - Routes to practitioner *before* client vents on Google/Instagram.
   - After a “doing great” response at the end of the journey, trigger:
     - Google/Shop review ask.
     - Rebooking link at the right interval.

5. **Pricing & Commitment**
   - Flat, transparent monthly fee tailored for small practices:
     - Target: **$79–99/mo** core tier.
   - **No setup fees. No 12‑month contracts.**
   - Early partners grandfathered at lower rates.

6. **Brand & Focus**
   - Everything – copy, examples, screenshots – speaks **aesthetics & tattoo**, not hospitals:
     - Botox, lips, filler, laser, brows, tattoos, piercings.
   - We are “for injectors and artists,” not “for all of healthcare.”

---

## Positioning Angles vs. Each Competitor

### vs Easy Aftercare

- “Easy Aftercare is built for hospitals and large clinics.  
  We’re built for **independent med spas and studios** that want the same power at a fraction of the price and setup time.”

- Talking points:
  - They: $149–399/mo + $299 setup, 12‑month contract.  
    We: ~$99/mo, no setup, cancel anytime.
  - They: multi‑specialty, heavy compliance, newsletters, surveys.  
    We: narrow, **aftercare + reputation + rebooking** only.
  - They: enterprise integrations (Zenoti etc.).  
    We: **Square/Vagaro** and small‑practice booking tools.

### vs PostCare.net (WhatsApp)

- “PostCare is WhatsApp‑first global; we’re SMS‑first for US med‑spa and tattoo clinics that don’t want to depend on WhatsApp.”

- Talking points:
  - They: manual enrollment after each treatment; staff picks protocol.  
    We: auto‑trigger from booking system when appointment is completed.
  - They: WhatsApp conversations and AI bots.  
    We: **simple, predictable SMS sequences** that don’t require clients to adopt a new app.
  - They: broad protocol library (good for them).  
    We: **smaller but deeper library** tuned specifically to med spa + tattoo/PMU, with strong defaults.

### vs Postcare.link

- “Postcare.link replaces paper with a better static link; we replace paper with **timed, proactive messages** that hit before clients panic.”

- Talking points:
  - They: single link with instructions.  
    We: scheduled SMS journey with reminders, check‑ins, and escalation.
  - They: clients must remember and open the link.  
    We: messages land automatically when it’s time.

---

## Strategic Guardrails

- **We will NOT try to:**
  - Compete for large hospital systems or plastic‑surgery‑only use cases.
  - Support every medical specialty from day one.
  - Build heavy EMR‑level features (charting, billing, detailed clinical documentation).

- **We WILL aim to:**
  - Be the **default aftercare tool** recommended among small med‑spa injectors and tattoo / PMU communities.  
  - Make setup so fast that a solo provider can go from signup to live journeys in **under 30 minutes**.  
  - Price the product so that **one prevented 1‑star review pays for a year** of subscription.
