# what do you mean by \$500 landing page

By “\$500 landing page” I mean:

You spend **about \$500 total on a simple landing page + small ad test** whose only job is to validate demand before you build the full product.

### What that actually includes

Rough breakdown (you can flex the numbers):

- **Domain + basic hosting/page builder**:
    - Domain: ~\$10–15/year
    - Landing page tool (Carrd, Framer, Webflow, Tally, etc.): ~\$15–30/month
- **Design / copy (optional)**:
    - Either you do it yourself (free) or pay a freelancer \$100–200 for a clean, credible design.
- **Paid traffic to test interest**:
    - ~\$300–400 on Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads targeting:
        - med spa owners, injectors, aesthetic nurses
        - tattoo/piercing studio owners and managers

Total ≈ **\$400–600**, I just said “\$500 landing page” as shorthand for “a small, contained validation experiment.”

### What the landing page should do

One simple page that:

- States the **problem** in their words (midnight aftercare DMs, bad reviews, paper instructions).
- Describes your **solution** (automated, timed aftercare texts, “something’s wrong” button, etc.).
- Shows **who it’s for** (solo injectors, tattoo studios, med spas).
- Has **one CTA**:
    - “Join the waitlist” or
    - “Apply for early access / free beta”

You then look at:

- Clicks from ads → landing page
- % of visitors who give you email / phone (conversion rate)
- Who they are (titles, shop types)

If, say, you spend \$400 on ads and get:

- 400 visitors
- 40+ signups (10%+)
- A bunch of people replying “yes, this is a real pain”

…that’s a strong enough signal to justify investing weeks building the MVP.
