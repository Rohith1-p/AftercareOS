# how much revenue are the current aftercare companies are doing?

No one publishes exact numbers for “aftercare automation” SaaS, but we can get good **bounds** from what’s public.

***

## Easy Aftercare (broad medical + aesthetics)

- Pricing: starts **\$149/month for up to 500 active patients**, higher tiers for 1,000–10,000+ patients, plus a **\$299 setup fee**.[^1]
- They integrate with Zenoti and market to many verticals (aesthetics, medspa, dental, ortho, plastic surgery, IVF, etc.), so they likely have **hundreds of clinics**, not just a few.[^1][^2]

Reasonable revenue range:

- If they had only **200 clinics** at an average \$200/month → **\$40K MRR (~\$0.5M ARR)**.
- If they’ve reached **1,000+ clinics** across all specialties → **\$200K+ MRR (~\$2.4M+ ARR)**.

Given:

- Broad vertical coverage (many specialties),
- Enterprise integrations (Zenoti),
- 12‑month contracts and setup fee,

it’s realistic they’re somewhere in the **mid‑six to low‑seven‑figure ARR** range, not tiny but not a giant yet.

***

## Misfit Tattoo / tattoo‑focused platforms

- Misfit (the broader marketing/tech company behind Misfit Tattoo) has **estimated annual revenue around \$8.2M** across its products/services.[^3]
- Only a portion of that is specifically “aftercare,” but it shows that **tattoo‑specific software can reach multi‑million annual revenue**.

***

## Market‑level signals

- **Tattoo aftercare products** alone are a **\$194.5M market in 2025**, projected to **\$374.2M by 2032 (9.8% CAGR)**.[^4]
- **Tattoo studio software** (booking, POS, etc.) is projected to reach **around \$1.2B by 2028** with ~8% CAGR.[^5]

Those categories are bigger than what you’re building, but they show that **multi‑million revenue businesses exist serving this ecosystem**, and there is budget for tools that improve healing, client experience, and operations.

***

## What this means for you

Based on current players:

- A focused aftercare SaaS (like your idea) that lands **a few hundred clinics/studios** can realistically get to **\$1M+ ARR**.
- Easy Aftercare proves **buyers will pay \$149–\$399/month** for strong aftercare automation.[^1]
- Misfit’s scale shows that in the tattoo world, a specialized platform can reach **multi‑million** revenue.[^3]

So your opportunity isn’t to be the first, but to:

- Serve **smaller, price‑sensitive studios and med spas** better, and
- Use **AI + simpler UX** to grow into that same low‑ to mid‑seven‑figure ARR range over time.
