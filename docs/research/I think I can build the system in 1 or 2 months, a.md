# I think I can build the system in 1 or 2 months, and in 2 months can't we get 100 customers at least, in this 2 months building process. we have to build a email list of customers

It’s technically possible, but getting **100 paying customers in your first 2 months** is very ambitious. Treat it as a stretch goal, not a plan.

Here’s a more realistic way to think about it.

***

## What’s realistic in 2 months?

If you:

- Spend 1–2 hours a day on outreach while building, and
- Have no existing audience in this niche,

then in the first 2 months you’re more likely to get:

- **20–40 qualified conversations**
- **10–20 serious beta users** (using it, maybe free/discounted)
- **5–10 paying customers** by the end of Month 2

Hitting **100+ paying** that fast usually requires:

- An existing big audience (YouTube/IG/TikTok/email) **or**
- Paid ads budget + sales help

So think:

- **Goal for Month 2:** 10 paying, 20–30 active users total
- **Goal for Month 6:** 50–100 paying

***

## Yes: Start building an email list now

While you build the product, your main job is to **build a list of future customers**.

### Step 1: Simple landing page

One page with:

- Headline: pain + promise
- Short explanation (what it does, who it’s for)
- Form: “Join early access” (email + studio type + booking software)

Use Carrd / Framer / Webflow + Tally / Typeform.

### Step 2: Traffic sources over 2 months

Aim to collect **200–300 emails** before launch. Rough numbers:

- **Cold DMs / email to studios**
    - Reach out to 10–20 per day
    - 10–20% may join the list
    - Over 2 months → 600–1,200 reached → 60–240 on list
- **Facebook / Reddit groups**
    - Share 1–2 helpful posts per week (no spam)
    - Add link to landing page in your profile / at the end of a genuinely useful post
    - Expect a slow trickle (maybe 20–50 signups)
- **Small paid test (optional)**
    - \$200–300 on Facebook/IG ads to “tattoo shop owners / med spa owners”
    - If your messaging is decent: 30–80 signups

***

## How the funnel might look

Assume:

- 250 people join waitlist
- 40% actually take a call or demo → ~100 demos
- 20–30% of demos convert to paid in first months → 20–30 paying

That’s **good** early traction. You can keep selling from there.

To get to **100 paying**:

- Either keep grinding this process for a few more months,
- Or layer on more aggressive marketing/sales later (bigger ads, partnerships, reseller deals, etc.).

***

## What to do during the 1–2 month build

While coding, keep doing **three parallel tracks** every week:

1. **Customer interviews**
    - 3–5 per week (tattoo + med spa)
    - Always end with: “Can I add you to the early access list?”
2. **Email list building**
    - Update list weekly with short progress email:
        - “Here’s what we’re building”
        - “Sneak peek of message sequences”
        - Ask 1 simple question (“What’s your biggest aftercare headache?”)
3. **Design partners**
    - Identify 5–10 studios willing to:
        - Use the product as soon as v1 is ready
        - Hop on calls every 1–2 weeks
        - Get “founding partner” pricing later

If you consistently do those three, you’ll be in a strong position to:

- Launch to **dozens** of warm leads (not strangers), and
- Convert **10–30** of them in the first couple of months post‑launch.
