# let's say I can join around 10 people at first, how much do you think we can charge? is it \$99?

For an initial group of ~10 early users, \$99/month is a **good target**, but I’d structure it slightly differently:

### 1. Anchor Price: \$99/month

Position your “normal” price as **\$99/month** for small studios/solo artists. That’s:

- Clearly cheaper than Easy Aftercare’s \$149+/month baseline.
- High enough that the product has to be valuable (you avoid “toy” pricing).


### 2. Early‑Partner Deal (for first 10)

For the first 10 shops you bring on, I’d offer:

- **First 3 months free** (or \$29/month) while you’re still iterating.
- Then **grandfathered at \$59/month** “forever” as a thank‑you.

This does a few things:

- Makes it very low risk for them to say “yes” early.
- Gives you room to later charge \$99–149/month for new customers while keeping your promises.
- Still gives you real revenue per user once you’re past the shaky MVP phase.


### 3. When to move to full \$99+

Once you see:

- They’re actively using it (e.g., sending 100+ aftercare journeys/month).
- At least 2–3 shops say “Honestly, I’d pay \$100+ for this.”

Then for **new** signups you can comfortably:

- Set standard pricing to **\$99/month** (tattoo / solo injectors)
- Add higher tiers (\$149–199) later for bigger med spas.

So: **aim for \$99 as the real price**, but use **\$0→\$59 “founding partner” pricing** for your first ~10 to reduce friction and get great case studies.
