# let's say we figured and understand that there is pain point and then built the system, how much revenue can we get based monthly?

If you validate the pain and ship a usable v1, your **monthly revenue depends almost entirely on how many studios you onboard and at what price**.

Here’s a clear range using realistic assumptions.

***

## 1. Pick a working price

Assume your “normal” SaaS price settles around:

- **Tattoo / small studios:** \$79–99/month
- **Med spa / injectors:** \$99–149/month

To keep it simple, use a blended **average of \$100/month per location**.

***

## 2. Revenue at different customer counts

Using \$100/month per paying account:


| Active locations | Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) | Annual Run Rate (ARR) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 10 | \$1,000/month | \$12,000/year |
| 25 | \$2,500/month | \$30,000/year |
| 50 | \$5,000/month | \$60,000/year |
| 100 | \$10,000/month | \$120,000/year |
| 250 | \$25,000/month | \$300,000/year |
| 500 | \$50,000/month | \$600,000/year |
| 1,000 | \$100,000/month | \$1.2M/year |

With the market size we discussed earlier (tens of thousands of eligible studios and med spas), landing **100–300 locations over a couple of years is very achievable**, which puts you in the **\$10K–\$30K MRR** band to start, with clear upside toward **\$50K+ MRR** if you keep executing.

***

## 3. What to aim for at each stage

- **Post‑MVP (first 6–9 months):**
    - Target: 30–50 paying locations → **\$3K–\$5K MRR**
- **Year 1 end (if things work):**
    - Target: 100–200 paying locations → **\$10K–\$20K MRR**
- **Year 2+:**
    - Target: 500+ locations → **\$50K+ MRR** (path to \$1M ARR)

These numbers assume:

- Churn stays reasonable (you keep most customers each month).
- You gradually raise price for later cohorts (early partners can be cheaper).
