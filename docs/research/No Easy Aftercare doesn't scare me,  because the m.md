## 1. How You Can Out-AI Easy Aftercare

Even though they mention “ADME” and ML, you can still differentiate with very concrete AI wins:

- **Protocol upload → sequence builder**
    - Take a PDF / screenshot of their current aftercare sheet.
    - AI extracts steps, groups by time (immediately, hours, days), and proposes a full SMS journey.
    - Provider just tweaks wording and hits “Activate.”
- **Per-service auto-personalization**
    - Same client gets fillers + Botox + microneedling → your system automatically merges journeys and de-duplicates messages (no spam).
- **Smart branching**
    - Clients respond to a check‑in (“How are you healing?”) with 1–5.
    - 1–3 → reassuring normal‑healing message; 4–5 → trigger “Something’s wrong” flow and route photos to provider.
- **Review + rebooking brain**
    - AI picks best time to ask for review (when NPS‑style response is positive) and to push rebooking based on procedure type and past behavior.

These are all very buildable with your current skill set plus LLMs.
