### **Week 3: Dig Into Data + Customer Conversations (Days 15-21)**

**Day 15-16: Analyze Ad Results**

**Target Metrics:**


| Metric | Good Sign | Red Flag |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **CTR** (Click-Through Rate) | 2%+ | <0.5% |
| **Landing Page Conversion** | 10%+ email signups | <5% |
| **CPC** (Cost Per Click) | <\$1 | >\$2 |
| **CPL** (Cost Per Lead) | <\$30 | >\$50 |
| **Total Signups** | 50+ | <20 |

**Decision Gate:**

- **Green Light:** 50+ signups + 10%+ conversion rate → Proceed to customer interviews
- **Yellow Light:** 20-50 signups or 5-10% conversion → Refine messaging, run again
- **Red Light:** <20 signups or <5% conversion → Your messaging isn't resonating, pivot or stop

***

**Day 17-21: Customer Discovery Interviews (5-10 calls)**

**Who to Interview:**

- People who signed up for waitlist (obviously!)
- Existing customers of Misfit Tattoo, Easy Aftercare (they know the problem)
- Owners you find on local Facebook groups

**Interview Script (30 min calls):**

1. **Problem Validation** (5 min)
    - "Tell me about your biggest challenge with aftercare right now"
    - "How often do clients text you after their appointments?"
    - "Ever had a bad review from something that was actually normal healing?"
2. **Solution Validation** (10 min)
    - "Imagine this: Automated texts sent at the right moments—Day 1, Day 3, Day 7. Would that solve your problem?"
    - "Would you use something like this? Why/why not?"
    - "What's the biggest concern you'd have?"
3. **Willingness to Pay** (5 min)
    - "If this existed, what would you pay? \$50/month? \$99? \$199?"
    - "Would one prevented bad review justify the cost?"
4. **Integration** (5 min)
    - "What booking system do you use?"
    - "Would you want it to automatically trigger from your booking system, or would you prefer to manually send it?"
5. **Next Steps** (5 min)
    - "Would you be willing to beta test this for free in 2-3 months?"
    - "Can I stay in touch?"

**Success Metric:** 8+ people saying "Yes, I'd use this" + \$99-199/month is acceptable

***

### **Week 4: Quick Market Research Deep Dive (Days 22-28)**

**Day 22-23: Competitive Intelligence**

- Sign up for Misfit Tattoo free trial → See how their aftercare works
- Sign up for Easy Aftercare demo (if possible) → Understand their approach
- Download Vagaro's "After Visit" email template → See how limited it is
- Review Zenoti pricing page → Confirm it's \$400+/month

**Questions to Answer:**

- Does Misfit actually solve aftercare well? Or are there gaps?
- Would providers switch from Misfit to your solution? Why?
- What could Easy Aftercare do that you can't?

**Day 24-25: Community Research**

- Join 3-5 Facebook groups:
    - "Medical Spa Owners \& Practitioners"
    - "Tattoo Shop Owners"
    - "Aesthetics Business Owners"
- Post (don't spam!): "What's your biggest pain point with patient aftercare?"
- Read responses → Document pain points in your own words

**Day 26-27: Search Trend Research**

- Google Trends: Search volume for "aftercare automation", "tattoo aftercare software", "med spa patient communication"
- Reddit: Search r/medicalspa, r/tattooadvice, r/aesthetics for aftercare complaints
- Identify: What are people actually searching for?

**Day 28: Synthesize Findings**

- Create one-page summary:
    - **What we learned:** List top 5 pain points from interviews
    - **Competitive gaps:** List what Misfit/Easy Aftercare/Zenoti don't do
    - **Willingness to pay:** What price did customers mention?
    - **Green lights:** Specific quotes of customers saying "I'd use this"
    - **Red flags:** Any concerns that came up repeatedly?

## 🎯 **Go/No-Go Decision (End of Week 4)**

### **Build It If:**

- ✅ 50+ landing page signups
- ✅ 8+ people said "I'd use this" in interviews
- ✅ Pricing feedback: \$99-199/month mentioned as acceptable
- ✅ Competitive gaps: Clear differentiation from Misfit/Easy Aftercare
- ✅ Integration feasibility: No technical blockers found


### **Pivot or Stop If:**

- ❌ <20 landing page signups (messaging not resonating)
- ❌ <5 people interested in interviews (not enough pain)
- ❌ Everyone wants free (\$99 not acceptable)
- ❌ Technical challenges blocking integrations
- ❌ A major competitor launches same feature during your validation
