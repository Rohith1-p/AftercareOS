## 🔍 **What Existing Platforms Currently Offer**

### **Square Appointments**

**What they have:**

- ✅ Pre-appointment reminders (24 hours before, 2 hours before, etc.)
- ✅ Appointment confirmations
- ✅ Cancellation/reschedule notifications
- ✅ Digital forms (intake forms after booking)
- ✅ Basic post-appointment feedback requests (via Square Feedback feature)

**What they DON'T have:**

- ❌ **Timed, sequential aftercare messages** (no "Day 1, Day 3, Day 7" messaging)
- ❌ **Procedure-specific aftercare protocols**
- ❌ **Automated aftercare instruction delivery**
- ❌ Community confirmation: Multiple users in Square forums asking "How do I send messages AFTER appointments?" and being told to use third-party tools

**Quote from Square Community (2025):**
> *"I'm trying to figure out if it's possible to send automated messages to customers AFTER their appointments are completed... I know you can configure reminders for upcoming appointments, but I can't seem to find an option for post-appointment messages."*

**Square's Response:** "Use a workaround like Zapier or Square Marketing automation"

***

### **Vagaro**

**What they have:**

- ✅ Pre-appointment reminders
- ✅ **After-visit campaigns** (can send ONE message immediately or up to 30 days after checkout)
- ✅ Text and email options
- ✅ Basic rebooking reminders

**What they DON'T have:**

- ❌ **Multi-message aftercare sequences** (only ONE after-visit message per campaign)
- ❌ **Day-by-day timed sequences** (can't do Day 1, Day 3, Day 7 automatically)
- ❌ **Procedure-specific messaging** (generic post-visit, not "Botox Day 1: don't lie down")
- ⚠️ **Workaround:** You'd have to create 5+ separate "After Visit" campaigns with different timing, manually manage which services trigger which campaigns

**What Vagaro's "After Visit" Actually Does:**

```
Service completed → Send ONE message X days later → Done
```

**What Your Platform Does:**

```
Service completed → Message 1 (5 min) → Message 2 (4 hours) → Message 3 (Day 1) → Message 4 (Day 3) → Message 5 (Day 7) → Message 6 (Day 14) → Message 7 (Month 3)
```

**Massive difference.**

**What they DON'T have (or it's unclear/limited):**
